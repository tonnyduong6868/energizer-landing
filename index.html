01The problem
why confluence usually fails

Adding indicators does not add confluence.
It adds arguments.

Every one of them is right sometimes. That is the trap — you can always find the two that agree with what you already want to do.

How it usually goes

Four indicators say buy, two say sell, and you pick the ones you already agreed with.
You add an SMC toolkit. Now the chart has so much on it you cannot see price.
The setup looked perfect. It was 03:00 and nothing was moving. You did not check.
You take TP1, the rest runs without you, and your journal calls it a win.
Sideways chop flips your signal every third bar and you keep taking them.

What the Energizer does instead

One number, 0-100, from trend, momentum and volatility. You argue with one thing.
A single Chart Density knob caps how much ink the script may spend. Clean means clean.
Killzone timing is worth +10 into the score. Off-session setups score lower on their own.
TP1, TP2 and TP3 are projected on entry and the stats book the R you actually finished on.
Sideway lockouts and anti-flip logic stop the engine changing its mind inside chop.

03Confluence
7 factors · capped at +30

Context does not sit in a second panel.

Most SMC tools show you the context and leave you to reconcile it with your entry signal by eye. Here the context is the score. Each factor below adds its points to the same 0–100 number — and can be turned into a hard gate that blocks the signal outright when the context is missing.

+30Combined cap

7 factors. Combined ceiling: +30. Stacking confluences cannot quietly inflate every setup into a 95 — the cap is in the source specifically to stop that. More evidence widens the base, it does not raise the roof.

  • +12Liquidity Sweep

    Price ran the last swing pivot and closed back inside. Stops taken, then rejected.

  • +10Killzone

    The setup landed inside the London / New York window. Reads the raw session clock, so hiding the boxes never changes a signal.

  • +10HTF FVG Mitigation

    Price returned into the most recent higher-timeframe fair value gap and respected it instead of closing through.

  • +8Displacement / FVG

    A three-bar imbalance left behind by an aggressive move.

  • +8Opening Gap Reaction

    Price traded into the live NDOG / NWOG and held the correct side of its midpoint. A gap narrower than one ATR counts as no gap — that keeps 24h markets from collecting this bonus every single bar.

  • +8MTF Alignment

    Higher timeframes agree with the direction. Only counts once you switch the filter on, so the confluence count never means two different things.

  • +6Open-Price Sweep

    Price ran one of the five session open levels (00:00 / 08:00 / 09:00 / 10:00 / 11:00) and closed back through it.

06Straight answers
the part nobody puts on a sales page

No countdown. No fake buyer popups. Here is why.

A timer that resets at midnight and a toast announcing a stranger in Madrid are not persuasion techniques, they are claims — and they are false ones. You are a trader. You notice. So instead, four things that are true and checkable.

We inflated our own stats. Then we fixed it.

In v1.0 the rolling Profit Factor booked TP1, TP2 and TP3 of the same trade as three separate winners. One 3R trade counted as +1R, +2R and +3R across three "trades". Trades replaced by an opposite signal, and trades that never resolved, vanished from the statistics entirely.

In v1.1 one closed trade produces exactly one record, at the R it actually finished on. Replaced and unresolved trades are closed at market and booked. Breakeven counts as half a win, not a full one.

Which means the profit factor you see in v1.1 is lower than the one v1.0 showed you. That is the point.

This does not predict anything

It scores the evidence in front of it. A 90 is a setup with a lot going for it, not a setup that is going to win. Nothing on a chart knows the future, and anything that tells you otherwise is selling you something worse than an indicator.

No backtest screenshot on this page

Every indicator sales page shows a curve going up. You cannot verify any of them, and neither can we. So instead: the script ships with a rolling profit factor that runs on YOUR chart, YOUR symbol, YOUR settings. Judge it there.

Signals do not repaint. The HTF FVG is built from closed bars only

That is a design constraint written into the source, not a promise in a FAQ. The higher-timeframe fair value gap that feeds the score reads closed HTF bars exclusively.

Seven confluences, capped at +30

Stacking confluences cannot quietly inflate every score past the ceiling. We built the cap in specifically so that adding more evidence widens the base without turning every setup into a 95.
